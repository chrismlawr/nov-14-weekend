Butter knives, Soho & sushi

A one-weekend pop-up on Upper Street, then Tala for Jess and a hunt for brown side-zip boots around Carnaby, and back to Angel for dinner at Guru Guru. The order to do it in, every shop with its Google rating, and the route.

Last updated 14 November 2026

Plan & order

The order runs west to east through Soho, then back to Regent Street, so nothing is crossed twice. Only three clock times matter today, and they are in coral.

If you run short of time
Drop Ronning and Our Legacy first. From Percival, lululemon is a 4-minute walk west.
Then Vuori. lululemon covers the same ground and you said it was the definite one.
No luck on boots? Office is open until 9pm, so you can go back for the Vagabonds after lululemon. Selfridges on Oxford Street also sells Vagabond, about 10 minutes west of Oxford Circus.
Red Wing is off the list: its boots are lace-up work boots, a different shape from your photos.

Allday Mini Mart

Pop-up shop · this weekend only

Allday Goods' two-day pop-up, dressed as an off-licence crossed with an old-school grocer. Ten limited-edition butter knives with handles made from partner brands' plastic waste: Kerrygold, Ottolenghi, Maldon, Bold Bean Co, Perelló and more.

OFFICE Carnaby Street

Boots · stocks Vagabond
4.2 ★★★★☆556 Google reviews

Your second photo is from Office's site and the boot is stamped Vagabond: a plain side-zip ankle boot with a rounded toe. Office carries Vagabond's men's boots, so this is the first place to try them on.
